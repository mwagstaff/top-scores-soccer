## Design Context

### Users
Football fans playing quick, touch-first arcade matches on iPhone or iPad. They want to choose a familiar club or national team, get into a match quickly, understand the result at a glance, and make meaningful progress in a longer competition without turning the game into a management simulation.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, approachable, and competitive. Match presentation should feel immediate and readable; major wins may become theatrical, triumphant moments without making routine screens visually noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
A native, portrait-first sports-game interface with clear hierarchy, football-led greens, strong team colours, and restrained rounded typography. Use both light and dark appearances where the existing app supports them. Reserve gold, stadium darkness, fanfare, and ticker tape for rare championship moments. Avoid generic neon gaming treatments, persistent decorative motion, and licensed visual likenesses the project does not own.

### Design Principles

- Keep setup and controls obvious enough for a three-minute arcade match.
- Make competition state, saves, and next actions unambiguous at a glance.
- Use one purposeful signature animation for exceptional outcomes rather than animating every screen.
- Preserve native controls, Dynamic Type, contrast, VoiceOver labels, and a meaningful Reduce Motion alternative.
- Keep effects performant on supported phones and tablets; decoration must never block input.
