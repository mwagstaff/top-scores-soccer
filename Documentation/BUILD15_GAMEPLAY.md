# Build 15: passing, high clearances and headers

**Build 15 is installed and launched on the connected iPhone.** Installation retained the app data.

**Receiver movement was revised in build 16:** any active joystick input now controls the receiver immediately; only a neutral stick enables automatic receiving movement. The build 15 behaviour below is historical.

## Controls to try

- **Pass:** aim roughly at a teammate and tap ACTION. All short taps use an available teammate; the slightly firmer tap becomes a through ball only when there is no teammate in the requested direction. Selection considers defenders in the lane and pressure on the receiver. Interceptions remain physical.
- **Receive:** control transfers to the intended teammate immediately. Neutral input, or the unchanged stick used to launch the pass, helps that player meet its flight path. A fresh direction takes over immediately. The receiver cushions the ball and settles it towards his feet; queue the return pass as usual.
- **Clear high and long:** in defence or midfield, aim upfield, hold ACTION for about **0.9–1 second**, then release. A moderate **0.4–0.6 second** hold produces a shorter lifted ball. At default tuning, an unmodified outfield kick first lands about **27–38 metres** away with a moderate hold, or **59 metres** away at full charge. Ratings, keeper foot ability, aftertouch and contact can change the result. Extra holding caps the clearance rather than introducing a shooting overhit penalty.
- **Shoot near goal:** within about **32 metres**, roughly goal-directed holds retain the shot meter's sweet spot, corner assistance and overhit risk. Elsewhere, held kicks follow the stick direction with useful loft. Fresh sideways input after a long kick still adds swerve.
- **Head:** when HEAD appears for a reachable aerial ball, aim with the joystick and tap ACTION. A tap up to about **half a second early** can prepare a header; direction is captured on release. The ball must physically reach the player's heading range. Waiting or missing does not cause a jump, slide, kick or vibration. A completed header has a brief hop and light impact feedback.
- **Contest high balls:** feet cannot collect aerial balls. Balls above heading range travel over outfield players; opponents can head a reachable descending ball. Keepers retain their own catching and diving rules. Offside also applies to headers, and a normal headed backpass can be handled by the keeper.
